How to Fix Android Phone Overheating (Causes and Quick Cool Down)

By ToolTutors Editorial Team • Updated: 2026-08-08 • 8 min read

How to Fix Android Phone Overheating (Causes and Quick Cool Down) — Holding a smartphone that feels like a hot pocket iron can be alarming. Excessive heat slows down your processor, causes frame drops in games, dims your screen brightness automatically, and permanently degrades your lithium-ion battery. Here is how to cool down your Android device immediately and prevent thermal throttling.

1. Emergency Steps to Cool Down a Hot Phone Immediately

If your phone displays a thermal warning or feels scorching hot:

  1. Remove the protective case: Thick silicone or leather cases trap thermal radiation like a winter jacket. Removing the case allows the aluminum and glass chassis to dissipate heat into ambient air.
  2. Unplug from the charger: Fast charging pumps high amperage into the battery. Unplug the cable immediately.
  3. Turn on Airplane Mode or Power Off: Disabling all cellular and Wi-Fi antennas drops CPU load to zero instantly.
  4. Place near a fan: Set the phone in front of an electric fan or cool air vent. Never put a phone in the refrigerator or freezer—rapid condensation can short-circuit internal motherboards!

2. The Top Culprits Behind Smartphone Overheating

Common triggers that push phone temperatures over 40°C (104°F):

  • Direct Sunlight: Leaving your phone on a car dashboard or beach towel absorbs radiant infrared heat quickly.
  • High-Framerate 3D Gaming: Running graphics-intensive titles (Genshin Impact, Call of Duty Mobile) at 60 FPS or 120 FPS pushes the GPU to its thermal threshold.
  • Using Phone While Fast Charging: Gaming or streaming 4K video while plugged into a 65W or 100W fast charger generates double the heat.
  • Rogue Background Camera or GPS Processes: A navigation app stuck searching for satellite locks keeps the GPS chip burning power.

3. Software Tweaks to Keep Your Phone Cool

Keep your phone running cool permanently:

  • Go to Settings > Display and reduce the refresh rate from 120Hz to standard 60Hz.
  • Turn on Auto-Brightness to avoid running maximum nits indoors.
  • Check Settings > Battery > Battery usage to identify and uninstall apps drawing abnormal percentages.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Never place an overheating phone in a freezer or ice bucket! The extreme temperature delta causes microscopic atmospheric moisture inside the chassis to condense into liquid water droplets on internal circuits.
