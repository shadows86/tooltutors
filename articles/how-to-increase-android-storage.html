How to Increase Android Storage Space Without Buying a New Phone

By ToolTutors Editorial Team • Updated: 2026-07-25 • 8 min read
Ad Space — 728×90

How to Increase Android Storage Space Without Buying a New Phone — Receiving the dreaded 'Storage space running out' warning prevents you from taking new photos, downloading app updates, and receiving WhatsApp messages. Before you spend hundreds of dollars on a new smartphone, follow these proven storage-reclaiming methods that easily free up 10GB to 30GB of space.

1. Identify the Biggest Storage Hogs

Don't guess what is consuming your space. Inspect your phone's internal storage breakdown:

  1. Open Settings > Storage.
  2. Review the category list: Apps, System, Images, Videos, Audio, and Documents.
  3. In 90% of phones, Videos and Apps account for 75% of all used gigabytes.

2. Purge Heavy WhatsApp Media and Memes

Group chats accumulate gigabytes of forwarded videos, voice notes, and duplicate memes:

  • Open WhatsApp > Settings > Storage and data > Manage storage.
  • WhatsApp displays a special category: Larger than 5 MB.
  • Review and delete old forwarded video clips you no longer need. This often frees up 5GB to 10GB in five minutes!

3. Use the 'Free Up Space' Tool in Google Photos

If your pictures are backed up to the cloud, there is no need to keep redundant local copies on your phone's internal storage: Open Google Photos > tap your profile avatar > tap Free up space. Google Photos safely removes photos from your phone that are already safely backed up in the cloud.

4. Expand with a High-Speed MicroSD Card

If your Android phone has an expandable MicroSD card slot, insert a fast Class 10 U3 / A2 MicroSD card. In Settings > Storage, format the card as portable storage for photos and music, or choose 'Adoptable storage' to store apps directly on the card.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid moving system-critical apps to cheap, slow MicroSD cards. Slow SD cards can cause app launch delays and stuttering animations. Use SD cards primarily for offline video, photos, and music files.
