How to Use Google Meet for Free: Host and Join Video Calls

By ToolTutors Editorial Team • Updated: 2026-08-18 • 7 min read
Ad Space — 728×90

How to Use Google Meet for Free: Host and Join Video Calls — Need a fast, reliable, and secure video calling platform without downloading heavy third-party software? Google Meet works directly inside Google Chrome, Edge, Safari, or on your smartphone. Every free Google account can host high-definition video calls with live closed captions. Here is how to get started.

1. How to Start an Instant Meeting

Hosting a meeting takes seconds:

  1. Open your web browser and navigate to meet.google.com (or click the Meet icon inside Gmail).
  2. Click the blue New meeting button.
  3. Choose from three options:
    • Create a meeting for later: Generates a reusable link to copy and paste into invitations.
    • Start an instant meeting: Immediately opens your webcam and room.
    • Schedule in Google Calendar: Sets up calendar reminders with automatic Meet links for all invitees.

2. How to Join a Google Meet Call

When someone invites you to a Google Meet, simply click the link (e.g., meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij). You will enter the 'Green Room' where you can test your microphone and check your hair on camera. Click Ask to join or Join now.

3. Screen Sharing and Real-Time Closed Captions

Two standout features make Google Meet exceptional:

  • Present Now (Screen Sharing): Click the icon with an arrow inside a rectangle. Choose A Chrome tab (best for smooth video and audio playback), A window, or Entire screen.
  • Live Captions (CC): Tap the CC icon at the bottom of the screen. Google's speech recognition displays real-time subtitles as people speak, invaluable for hearing accessibility or noisy environments.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Do not use 'Entire Screen' when presenting sensitive material. Sharing a single Chrome tab prevents meeting attendees from seeing your private bookmarks, incoming Slack notifications, or personal email tabs.

Frequently Asked Questions

One-on-one calls can last up to 24 continuous hours. Group meetings with 3 or more people are free for up to 60 minutes per session.

If the host has a personal Google account, attendees must log into a Google account for security. If the host has Google Workspace, outside guests can join without one.

Cloud meeting recording is reserved for paid Google Workspace business accounts. However, free users can take notes or capture screen summaries locally.
