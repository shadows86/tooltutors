How to Share Wi-Fi Password on Android (QR Code and Quick Share)

By ToolTutors Editorial Team • Updated: 2026-08-04 • 6 min read
Ad Space — 728×90

How to Share Wi-Fi Password on Android (QR Code and Quick Share) — Remembering a long, complex 24-character home Wi-Fi password with random numbers and special symbols is nearly impossible. Fortunately, Android includes a brilliant built-in feature that lets you share your Wi-Fi credentials with guests via an instant QR code or wireless beam. Here is how to share access in 10 seconds without speaking a single letter.

1. How to Generate a Wi-Fi QR Code on Android

As long as your Android smartphone is currently connected to the network:

  1. Open the Settings app on your Android handset.
  2. Tap Network & internet > Internet (or Connections > Wi-Fi on Samsung).
  3. Tap the gear icon next to the network you are currently connected to.
  4. Look for the button labeled Share (it displays a small QR code icon).
  5. Verify your identity by scanning your fingerprint or entering your lock screen PIN.
  6. A prominent, high-contrast QR code will appear on your screen, with your Wi-Fi password printed in plain text directly beneath it!

2. How Your Guests Connect Instantly

Connecting is effortless for both Android and iPhone users:

  • iPhone Users: Open the standard iOS Camera app, point it at your Android's screen, and tap the yellow 'Join Network' banner that pops up.
  • Android Users: Open the Camera app or pull down Quick Settings and tap the QR code scanner tile, point at the screen, and tap Connect.

3. Beam Wirelessly via Quick Share

On devices running modern Android, tap the Quick Share button located directly underneath the QR code. If your friend has an Android phone nearby with Bluetooth enabled, their phone name will pop up. Tap their device, and their phone will connect to your Wi-Fi automatically without even scanning!

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Never share Wi-Fi credentials with untrusted public strangers. Guests on your local network can theoretically access shared network folders or smart home speakers.
